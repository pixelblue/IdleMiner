# GDD — Idle Miner

## Concept
- Idle/incremental game with an isometric top-down view
- No player character — player controls only the mouse cursor
- Mine asteroids, satellites and space debris to gather resources
- Goal: craft and terraform your own asteroid and make it green

## Core Loop
- Hover cursor over space objects to mine them (early game, manual)
- Build structures on your asteroid to automate and scale
- Refine raw resources into better materials
- Use materials to grow a living, green base on your asteroid

## Progression
- Early: manual mining, place first Drone Spawner
- Mid: drones automate mining, Refinery unlocks
- Late: asteroid transforms from bare rock to lush green world

## Key Structures
- Drone Spawner
- Resource Container
- Refinery

## Resource Tiers
- Tier 1: Raw Ore
- Tier 2: Refined Metals
- Tier 3: Biological / Living Materials
